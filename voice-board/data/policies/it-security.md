# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements
Employees must use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols) and enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all company accounts. Passwords must be updated every 90 days.

## Device Management
Company-issued devices must not be shared with unauthorized individuals, including family members. Devices must be locked when unattended. Employees must immediately report any lost or stolen devices to the IT Helpdesk.

## Data Protection
Confidential company data must only be stored on approved internal systems or cloud drives. Sending sensitive information (like PII, financial data, or trade secrets) to personal email addresses is strictly prohibited.

## Software Installation
Only approved software from the IT self-service catalog may be installed on company devices. Unauthorized software, browser extensions, or pirated media will be automatically blocked or removed.
