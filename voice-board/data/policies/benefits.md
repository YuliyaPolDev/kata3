
# Benefits Policy

## Health Insurance
Eligibility and plan options depend on employment status and local regulations. We offer comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage. Open enrollment occurs annually in November, during which employees can adjust their coverage plans.

## Parental Leave
Parental leave is available to eligible employees per the policy rules for your region. Primary caregivers are entitled to up to 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and secondary caregivers are entitled to up to 6 weeks.

## Retirement Plans
The company offers a robust 401(k) matching program to help you plan for the future. We match 100% of employee contributions up to the first 4% of eligible compensation.

## Wellness Programs
Employees have access to a monthly wellness stipend of $100 that can be used for gym memberships, mental health apps, or at-home fitness equipment.
