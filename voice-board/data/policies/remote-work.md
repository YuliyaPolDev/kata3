# Remote Work Policy

## General Guidelines
Our company supports a flexible work environment. Employees may be eligible to work remotely depending on their role, departmental requirements, and manager approval.

## Home Office Setup
Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, quiet, and productive remote workspace. A one-time stipend of $500 is provided for home office equipment (such as monitors, ergonomic chairs, and desks) upon hire.

## Core Hours and Availability
Remote employees are expected to be online and available during core business hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time) to facilitate cross-team collaboration and meetings. Flexible hours outside of this window can be arranged with your manager.

## Internet and Security
Employees must ensure a reliable internet connection. All work must be conducted over a secure, private network, utilizing the company VPN when accessing internal services.
