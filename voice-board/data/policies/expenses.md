# Expense Policy

## Covered Expenses
The company will reimburse employees for reasonable and necessary business-related expenses incurred during authorized travel or operations. Examples include flight tickets, hotel accommodation, ground transportation, and client meals.

## Submission Process
All expense reports must be submitted through the company's expense management software within 30 days of the expense date. Itemized receipts are required for all expenses over $25.

## Per Diem and Meals
When traveling for business, employees are granted a daily per diem rate for meals. The standard per diem rate varies by location and can be found in the Travel Portal.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal expenses, fines for traffic violations or parking tickets, in-room movies, and unapproved flight or hotel upgrades will not be reimbursed.
