# Employee Handbook

## Introduction
Welcome to our company! This handbook provides an overview of our foundational policies, procedures, and expectations to help you succeed in your role.

## Time Off
Employees should request planned time off through the standard HR portal as early as possible. We offer a flexible Paid Time Off (PTO) policy, meaning there is no strict cap on vacation days, provided performance goals are met and absence is approved by your manager.

## Sick Leave
In addition to flexible PTO, employees are provided with 10 paid sick days per year for personal health or the care of a family member.

## Workplace Conduct
We expect respectful communication and professional behavior at all times. Professionalism extends to both in-office interactions and digital communications (e.g., Slack, email, video calls, internal comments).

## Performance Reviews
Performance is reviewed bi-annually. These sessions are intended to provide constructive feedback, align on strategic goals, and discuss long-term career progression.
