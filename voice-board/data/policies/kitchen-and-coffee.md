# Kitchen and Coffee Etiquette

## The Coffee Machine
Our office is equipped with a high-end espresso machine, as well as a standard drip coffee maker. We encourage everyone to enjoy these amenities! If you empty the beans or the water tank, please refill them for the next person. If you are unsure how to use or clean the espresso machine, ask the office manager for a quick tutorial.

## Snacks and Beverages
The company provides a variety of free snacks, fresh fruit, and beverages (sparkling water, sodas, juices) in the kitchen area. Please be mindful and take only what you need so there is enough for everyone. 

## Fridge Rules and Cleanliness
Please label your personal food items with your name and the date. The office refrigerators are cleaned out every Friday at 4:00 PM. Any unlabeled or expired items will be thrown away. 

## General Kitchen Etiquette
Your mother doesn't work here! Please wash your own mugs, plates, and utensils immediately after use, or place them directly in the dishwasher. Wipe down the counters or microwave if you make a spill. Let's keep our shared space clean and welcoming for everyone.
