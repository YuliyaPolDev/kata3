# Hobbies and Employee Clubs

## Employee-Led Clubs
We believe that connecting over shared interests makes us a stronger team. Employees are encouraged to start and join company clubs. Current active clubs include the Book Club, the Running Club, the Board Game Society, and the Gardening Group.

## Club Budgets
Any group of three or more employees who wish to formalize a club can apply for a small quarterly budget. This budget can be used to purchase books, group snacks, shared tools, or to subsidize event tickets. Requests should be submitted to the HR department at the start of each quarter.

## Hobby Channels
We have dedicated Slack channels for various hobbies where you can share your passions, ask for advice, or just show off your latest project. Popular channels include `#dogs-of-the-office`, `#cat-corner`, `#baking-and-cooking`, `#plant-parents`, and `#gaming`.

## Show and Tell Sessions
On the last Friday of every month, we host a casual "Show and Tell" session over lunch (or via video call for remote employees). This is a great opportunity to present a recent hobby project, talk about a trip you took, or teach your coworkers a new skill.
