# Code of Conduct

## Anti-Harassment
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any form will not be tolerated. Our workplace is a safe environment, where every individual must feel respected, supported, and valued.

## Reporting Procedures
Employees can report concerns to HR through the established reporting channels or anonymously via our third-party hotline. We strictly prohibit retaliation against anyone who reports an incident in good faith.

## Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid any situation that involves a conflict between their personal interests and the interests of the company. Gifts or entertainment from vendors should be reported to management if their value exceeds $100.

## Intellectual Property
All work produced by employees during working hours, using company resources, remains the sole property of the company. Unauthorized sharing of internal source code, customer data, or business strategies is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.
