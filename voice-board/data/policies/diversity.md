# Diversity and Inclusion Policy

## Our Commitment
We are deeply committed to fostering a workplace that celebrates diversity and ensures equal opportunities for all employees, regardless of race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or background.

## Anti-Discrimination
Discrimination or bias in hiring, promotion, compensation, or daily interactions is strictly prohibited. We believe that diverse perspectives drive innovation and success.

## Inclusive Hiring Practices
Our recruitment processes are intentionally designed to mitigate unconscious bias and ensure we attract and fairly evaluate candidates from diverse backgrounds. This includes diverse interview panels and standardized assessment criteria.

## Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
The company proudly supports several Employee Resource Groups. These groups provide safe spaces for networking, mentorship, and advocacy. Employees are encouraged to join or start new ERGs aligned with their identities or allyship.
