# Internal Communication and Socializing

## Chat Channel Guidelines
We use our internal chat platform (e.g., Slack/Teams) for both work and play. Please keep work-related discussions in their designated project channels. For casual conversations and socializing, use the `#watercooler`, `#random`, or specific hobby channels.

## Respecting Focus Time
While we value an open and communicative culture, we also respect deep work. If a colleague's status is set to "Do Not Disturb," "In a Meeting," or "Focusing," please refrain from sending non-urgent messages. Use scheduled messages for the next working day if possible.

## Virtual Coffee Chats
To help integrate remote workers and foster cross-departmental relationships, we use an automated system that randomly pairs employees for a 15-minute "Virtual Coffee" every two weeks. Participation is completely optional but highly encouraged as a way to meet people you wouldn't normally work with.

## Social Events and Happy Hours
We frequently organize team lunches, after-work happy hours, and virtual game nights. Attendance at these social events is always voluntary. We strive to ensure our events are inclusive, offering non-alcoholic beverage options and scheduling events at times that accommodate various time zones and personal commitments.
